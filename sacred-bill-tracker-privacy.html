Sacred Bill Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

Sacred Bill Tracker for Android version 1.0.9 is a local-first bill, income, focus, schedule, to-do, calendar, reports, and CPA export app.

Information Stored Locally

The Android app stores bill details, due dates, categories, recurrence settings, paid status, notes, income entries, Sacred Focus tasks, schedule blocks, to-dos, partner-preparation data, and export-related records locally on your device.

How Data Is Used

Local data is used to show bills, dashboard totals, reminders, calendar views, reports, weekly income summaries, Sacred Focus planning, schedule blocks, and CPA export files when you choose to export.

Google Play Billing

Optional Pro and Focus Plus subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing. Google Play processes purchases, renewals, cancellations, and subscription management. The Android app does not receive or store your payment card information.

CPA Exports

CPA exports are created locally on your device as ZIP/CSV files. The app opens the Android share sheet only when you choose to share an export. You control where exported files are sent or saved.

Cloud Sync Status

Cloud Sync is not active in Android version 1.0.9 unless a secure backend is configured in a future release. This Android build does not include backend credentials, and it does not send bill or income data to a sync server.

Network And Accounts

Core bill tracking works locally without an account. Internet access may be used for Google Play Billing and Android or Google Play system services. Sacred Bill Tracker does not connect to bank accounts, move money, pay bills, provide loans, or provide financial, legal, accounting, or tax advice.

Data Deletion

You can edit or delete bills, income entries, focus tasks, and schedule items inside the app where those controls are available. You can delete local Android app data by uninstalling Sacred Bill Tracker or clearing the app storage in Android system settings. You can delete CPA export files from wherever you saved or shared them. Subscriptions can be canceled or managed in Google Play.
